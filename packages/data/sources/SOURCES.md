# Sources of the world

What feeds `clubs-hdf.csv` and the reference tables, and what does not. Rules: `docs/DONNEES-ET-LEGAL.md` §2.

## Used

| Data                                           | Source                                                                               | Licence / terms                            |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| Districts, divisions and their depth           | Wikipédia, « Ligue de football des Hauts-de-France » (read on 2026-09-29)            | CC BY-SA 4.0                               |
| Clubs of the pilot, their commune and division | Compiled by hand from public pages, then **checked by a person** (`verified` column) | Facts about associations                   |
| Communes and INSEE codes                       | geo.api.gouv.fr / INSEE                                                              | Licence Ouverte 2.0                        |
| Grounds and their surfaces                     | Recensement des équipements sportifs (data.gouv.fr)                                  | Licence Ouverte 2.0 (to check on download) |
| Associations of the department                 | Répertoire national des associations (data.gouv.fr)                                  | Licence Ouverte 2.0 (to check on download) |

Kit colours, patterns and crests are **not** sourced from clubs' logos: colours a person knows are marked
`known`; the others are a deterministic guess (`guess`) to correct. Crests are generated by the game.

## Not used (and why)

- **The FFF's competition API** (`api-dofa.fff.fr`) and its successor `epreuves.fff.fr`: not public,
  protected by a bot-detection firewall (Akamai) since 2026; the only ways in described online are
  workarounds with automated browsers. Excluded.
- Transfermarkt, Flashscore and similar: terms forbid automated extraction.
- Any logo or photo found online.
- Summaries of web pages produced by a language model: they mix up leagues and invent lines. A line
  of the CSV comes from a page a person read, or from a data file parsed by a script.
